# Security

To report a vulnerability in private, open a GitHub security advisory:

**https://github.com/lynnarize/liner-code/security/advisories/new**

Do not open a public issue, and do not post the details in Discussions. The advisory form is the
private channel to Lin ([@lynnarize](https://github.com/lynnarize)). It is available once private
vulnerability reporting is on for this repository.

Say which version you ran (the app, or [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md)), what you did, and what
happened. A crash or a bug that is not a vulnerability is a
[bug report](https://github.com/lynnarize/liner-code/issues/new?template=bug.yml). **Help › Report
a Problem…** in the app starts that form. Look through the log before you attach it: it can hold
folder names and parts of what the agents did.

What the app sends off the machine, and the threat model, are in the README ("Your accounts and
your data") and in [SECURITY_MODEL.md](SECURITY_MODEL.md).
