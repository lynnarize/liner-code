## Next (not released)

Notes for the next release are collected here, then moved into `CHANGELOG.md` when it ships
(the website is built from `CHANGELOG.md`).

- Moving a session to another tool's model hands it a note with everything so far: who answered what, the first ask, the plan left open, the files changed and how a failed command or a stopped turn ended. The chat shows "Switched" with both models and the effort, and opens on the note it was sent; before you send, a line says your next message hands off.
- Every Hermes model offers Hermes's effort levels, not only the one a session last ran on: Liner Code checks whether an agent's levels are the same whatever the model, and a model id you add to such an agent takes them too. A model without levels now says whether its tool lists none for it or hasn't said yet.
- OpenCode Go's limits no longer read the Go key OpenCode or Hermes saved until you agree: Read the Go key in Usage, or Settings › General.
- The Effort pill is empty at a model's lowest level and full only at its highest: the lower of two levels (OpenCode's None) no longer draws as half. Its fill ends round about the knob.
- Claude Code's and Codex's model lists show the models alone, without a line over each saying what it is for.
- The model picker opens with its list over the chip you clicked and the card about a model beside it, to the right. Before, the list stood a card's width away to the left and only the small card was over the chip.
- The session's surfaces are a row of icons at the right end of its header (Session, Terminal, Changes, Browser, Files, ···); the rail down the paper's right edge and the panel's tabs are gone.
- The right panel is a flush column of the paper with one hairline at its left, every surface has one head with its name, Full canvas and Close, and in the full canvas or a narrow window the session header stays in view above it.
- Every menu has one shape: 28px rows, a 14px grey icon, the value or tick at the right end, grey labels and foot lines, and rows that open a menu to the side.
- A working mark is three dots travelling round in grey whoever's model it is; in lists of steps a finished step is a tick, one not started an empty ring, a failed one a red cross.
- In Liner Security a finding and a scan's Found panel have the right panel's frame: a head with the name, how bad and Close; Summary, Code and Activity as sections of one column; a foot with Fix in ink last.
- Liner Security's find fields are always there and say "Find a finding", "Find a project", "Find a session"; Findings' order and exports share one options menu.
- Liner Security's empty states, menus and step marks are the app's own, and each page's one action (Add a project, Start a scan, Dismiss them) is ink.
- Pull request, Design, Side chat and add-on panels sit in the panel's one frame, each with one action in ink in its foot.
- Linked pull requests is now Pull request's "#N" picker, which its key opens; an add-on's ··· can pin it to Your apps.
- Pointing at a session in the sidebar shows Pin, Settle and ···, whose menu adds Mark unread and Stop it, with Snooze until and Group opening to the side.
- The Session popup says where a session runs, what fills its context and its setup as rows, with Multi-agent, Tool preset, Connections, Spend limit and Workspace memory each a menu to the side.
- The crew chip and the Swarm or Teamwork chip open one agents popup: who works as a tree, the board and the worktrees, then Pause, Hibernate all and Turn it off.
- The Plan and the team at work are cards at the end of the chat, over the live line, each folding to one line; the strips over the message box are gone.
- The live line is one line: the newest step, its time and ›.
- A team's report to its coordinator reads as one folded row, who to whom and its first sentence, that opens in place.
- One agent in the panel has a head with a way back, who sent it and Stop, and its message box as the foot.
- Changes keeps its turn, its files as "N files ⌄", its totals and Previous and Next in its head; how the diff is drawn is one options menu, each file's row has a bare Viewed tick and your editor, and the foot says what you've viewed beside Undo and Commit in ink.
- Asking about lines in Changes' split view is the same box as in the unified view.
- Files has its find field, Only changed files and one Look again in its head, a file's Copy path, Copy text and Open in Browser under its ···, and Show changes and Open in editor in its foot.
- The Browser's address field holds Back, Forward and Reload, and pressing the address lists the folder's dev servers (Run, Restart, Stop), its pages and the other servers on this Mac; Width, Theme and Zoom are rows of its ··· that open to the side, and the page fills the panel edge to edge.

- Liner Design's top row has the session header's buttons, Build in a project and Another folder open to the side, and the running app's picker lists dev servers as the Browser does.
- A design's row menu has Stop it while it draws, Rename with F2 and its deletes in red; deleting a page asks once more.
- The pinned Design chat has the panel's head, and the small chat row is one line ending in ›.
- Settings, Usage, Setup and the Add-ons page take the panel's look: each form's one action is in ink, a picture switch list and Add one's other ways are menus, an add-on's details read as a panel with Remove in its ···, and Setup's list wears the step marks.
