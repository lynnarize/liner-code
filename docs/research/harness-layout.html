  • Three layers of tabs. "Session views" in the header, the dock's strip (one per pane, two panes at most), then tabs inside a surface: Shells in Terminal, segmented controls in Files, Browser, Pull request, Design and Review.
  • Bars stack up. Session header, dock strip, the surface's own bar, sometimes Shells. Full-canvas Review repeats the title and branch in a header of its own (Review.tsx:367).
  • Five or six doors to one surface. A header button, ⋯ "More surfaces", the + guide, ⌘K, a key, and for the diff the laptop menu too. "Hide the dock" sits in two places.
  • One thing, two names. "Changes" in the header and the laptop menu, "Diff" as a tab and in ⋯.
  • A sidebar of mixed parts. An action bar, the Add-ons menu, Apps, groups, projects, a Snoozed and Settled tray, then five buttons in the footer. Each row takes two lines and repeats its project.

Rules for the new shell

  1. One header per column, 52 px, nothing stacked on it.The panel's tab strip is the panel's header, and it lines up with the session's.
  2. One layer of tabs.A surface gets one tool row at most, and no tabs of its own.
  3. One door you click, one key.Every surface opens from the panel's + or ⌘⇧ and its letter. ⌘K finds everything.
  4. One name per thing, in every menu.Changes, Terminal, Pull request: the tab, the button and ⌘K say the same word.
  5. Rows on one line.What needs a second line goes in the hover card.
  6. Sections show only while they have rows.Needs you, Pinned and the shelves come and go.

Kept as it is

  • The Reviewer. The Reviewing, Verified and Blocked badge stays in the header, and the chip under a turn stays where it is.
  • The turn layout from 1 November. A head per model's part ("Opus 5.5 · planned in 41s"), grey folded rows, and a permission request that never folds.
  • Bare models by default. Named agents show only when you made one.
  • Four permissions. Auto, Manual, Reviewer decides, then Full access.
  • Home's strip, option C, and the quiet line under it.
  • The composer's box. Its shape, its placeholder and the round red Send, on Home and in a session. Only the buttons in its tray change.
  • The Desk, hidden. The cue at Home's foot ("2 back · 2 out"), and two scrolls to slide it up.
  • Add-ons as built on 3 November. The three places for an app, the "Make it an app" card, sealed frames, the strip of what each one touches, holding to allow network, and kept versions.
  • Run, the prompts map, ⌘K and Settings, and Liner 2.0 throughout: flat surfaces, one accent, red for the main button, no glass.
