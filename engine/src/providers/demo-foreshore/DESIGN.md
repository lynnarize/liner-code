# Foreshore, tide tables

The brand, the place and the numbers are made up: this is Liner Code's demo.

The day is drawn the way a sea chart draws a coast.
The time a sailor needs is the largest thing on screen.

## Concept

- Idea: the tide curve is a coastline, and the screen is coloured by what is under water at that hour.
- Borrowed from: the sea chart. Buff land, green foreshore that dries, blue shallows, white deep water, magenta marks, small sloping figures for depths.
- Signature: the day as one section, land above high water, the foreshore uncovered above the curve, water below it, the time of high water standing on the high-water line.
- The frame to show: Desktop / Today.

## Colour, taken from the chart

- Deep       #F7F8F5   the page, and deep water
- Shallows   #BFE0EA   water over the foreshore
- Foreshore  #B5D3A0   ground that dries at low water
- Land       #F0DA9C   above high water
- Ink        #11181B   lines, type, the coastline
- Mark       #C4127A   now, lights, springs. Nothing else

Night: Deep #0C1315, Shallows #143A4A, Foreshore #27452E, Land #463C1B, Ink #E7ECE8, Mark #FF5FB0.

## Type: two voices, as on a chart

Land stands upright: Archivo, for names and controls.
Water slopes: times and heights in Archivo italic (width 62 to 80, weight 700 to 800),
"high water" and "low water" in Newsreader italic.
Figure 172 · Water 34 · Body 15 · Label 11 caps, width 125, tracked 0.14em

## Space and shape

Steps of 4: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32. Blocks pad 24.
Corners 4. Hairlines in ink at 16%. No outlines, no shadows.
The foot of a chart carries the hour scale: one block an hour, alternating.

## Never

- Magenta as decoration
- A time of tide that stands upright
- 12-hour times

## The mark

A ring, one tide through it, and a magenta dot where the curve crosses the centre: the moment you are in.
Line: "Twice a day, exactly."
